# 🛍️ Abdi Online Shopping — Customer AI Assistant
## Comprehensive Capabilities & Question Guide for Customers & Shoppers

---

### 🌟 Executive Overview

The **Abdi AI Assistant** is an intelligent, 24/7 personal shopping assistant and customer support copilot embedded directly into the Abdi Online Shopping storefront. It empowers Ethiopian shoppers to browse products, check real-time stock and prices, track personal orders, verify payments, get doorstep delivery estimates to their specific village, and receive tailored product recommendations in fluent English or natural Amharic.

#### Key Superpowers:
* ⚡ **Live Catalog & Stock Sync**: Pulls real-time prices, availability, and out-of-stock statuses directly from the store database without needing a page refresh.
* 📦 **Personal Device Order Tracking**: Automatically recognizes the customer's device orders, tracks real-time order status, and explains Telebirr approval/rejection notes transparently.
* 🛍️ **Interactive Product Cards**: Attaches 1-click **"Order Now"** and **"View Details"** cards directly in the chat with product photos, prices in ETB, and live stock badges.
* 🔤 **Smart Typo & Phonetic Tolerance**: Understands common spelling mistakes (e.g., *"oder"*, *"paymant"*, *"telebir"*, *"availble"*, *"buanbua"*, *"cood"*).
* 🇪🇹 **Full Bilingual English & Amharic (አማርኛ)**: Shoppers can converse naturally in English or Amharic (ጽሁፍ ወይም በድምጽ ማዘዝ ይችላሉ).
* 🛡️ **Customer Trust & Anti-Scam Focus**: Reassures customers regarding original quality guarantees, doorstep inspection before payment (Cash on Delivery), and official hotline access.
